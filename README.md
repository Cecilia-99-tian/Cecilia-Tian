# Cecilia Tian

I work on expert-driven AI data, complex task design, and model evaluation, with a focus on bringing human expertise into the development of more capable and trustworthy AI.

My interests also span simulation assets, world models, and embodied intelligence. I'm particularly interested in how data, simulated environments, and real-world evaluation can work together to improve the way intelligent systems learn and act.

## What I focus on

**Expert data & expert collaboration**

Translating domain knowledge and expert judgment into well-defined tasks, useful training data, and consistent quality standards. I care about how experts work together, resolve disagreements, and develop a shared understanding of quality.

**Complex tasks & trustworthy AI**

Designing realistic tasks, clear evaluation rubrics, and review workflows to understand what models can do, where they fail, and what successful task completion actually means.

**Simulation, world models & embodied intelligence**

Exploring how simulation assets and world models can support learning and evaluation, and how capabilities developed in simulated environments hold up in the physical world.

## What I plan to share

I plan to use this space to share practical frameworks, task examples, evaluation templates, and experiments as they develop.

I'm especially interested in exchanging ideas with people working on AI data, evaluation, simulation, and embodied intelligence, as well as people bringing expertise from other disciplines.

## Beyond work

My curiosity extends well beyond AI. I enjoy exploring unfamiliar subjects, following unexpected connections, and learning how other people see the world.

Whether we share a field or simply share a curiosity, you're welcome to say hello.
